About the Conference

Professor C.C. Nwosu
Associate Vice President
Institutional Effectiveness.

The International Conference for Faith Integration is organized by the Institute for Faith Integration, domiciled in the Office of Institutional Effectiveness at Babcock University, in furtherance of its vision to promote faith integration for the holistic development of human capacity. As a Seventh-day Adventist Institution with a unique educational perspective, the integration of faith at all levels of education and profession is pivotal to Babcock University’s course of promoting and inculcating the Christian world view in students and every worker.

As an institution established to serve as a tower and edifice of truth to the world, Babcock University is positioned to advance the frontiers of faith integration beyond its walls. Hence, the organization of an international conference for faith integration. Through this conference, where participants will be drawn from within and outside the country, Babcock University will be fulfilling the prophetic mandate of Isaiah 58:12
