Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit my abstract and full paper?+
Send your 250-word abstract in Times New Roman 12pt (.doc or .docx) to icfi@babcock.edu.ng by March 24, 2027. Full papers are due May 9, 2027.
How does the ICFI Member ₦10,000 discount work?+
Registered ICFI community members receive ₦10,000 off the standard ₦50,000 fee and retain Early Bird rates (₦40,000 / $80) year-round, including after April 23, 2027.
Is campus accommodation available for visiting delegates?+
Yes—campus and partner accommodation is available for an additional fee. Contact Dr. Nnenna Nwobi at +234 813 538 8049 for room arrangements.
