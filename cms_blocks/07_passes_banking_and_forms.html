1. Important Membership Information and Conditions

IMPORTANT MEMBERSHIP INFORMATION AND CONDITIONS

By registering with the International Community for Faith Integration (ICFI), applicants acknowledge and agree to the following:

Annual Membership Fee: Membership of the International Community for Faith Integration attracts an annual subscription fee of US$5 (approximately ₦7,000). The fee is payable upon registration and is renewable annually to maintain active membership. The applicable Naira equivalent may be adjusted to reflect prevailing exchange rates.

Membership Renewal: Membership is valid for one year from the date of registration and must be renewed annually to retain active membership status and its associated privileges.

Voting Rights and Participation in Governance: Registered members whose memberships are active and up to date are entitled to voting rights in matters requiring decisions by the Community. Members may be called upon to participate in periodic virtual meetings to deliberate on proposals, vote on resolutions, and contribute to decisions concerning the administration, programmes, and general affairs of the Community.

Participation in Virtual Meetings: Members are expected to make reasonable efforts to attend scheduled virtual meetings and participate in the Community’s decision-making processes. The Community will communicate meeting notices, agendas, and relevant information to members in advance.

Concessions on Paid Events: Registered members are entitled to preferential considerations, including discounted registration fees or other applicable concessions, for selected paid programmes and events organised by the Community, such as the International Conference for Faith Integration (ICFI). The nature and extent of such concessions will be communicated ahead of each event.

Membership Privileges and Eligibility: Membership privileges, including voting rights and event concessions, are available only to members whose annual subscriptions are current. Members with expired subscriptions may be required to renew their membership before accessing these privileges.

Commitment to the Community’s Objectives: Members are expected to uphold the vision, mission, values, and objectives of the International Community for Faith Integration and to conduct themselves in a manner consistent with the Community’s commitment to advancing faith integration in scholarship, professional practice, and institutional life.

Communication and Updates: Members are expected to provide accurate contact information and remain accessible through the official communication channels designated by the Community. The Community will communicate important announcements, meeting invitations, programme updates, and membership-related information through these channels.
