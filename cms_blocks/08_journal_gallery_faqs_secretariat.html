Frequently Asked Questions

How do I arrange campus accommodation?+

On-campus lodging (Babcock Guest House, Havillah Cottage, or Royal Cream Suite) is available for an additional fee. Contact Dr. Nnenna Nwobi at +234 813 538 8049.

How does the ₦10,000 community member discount work?+

Registered members of ICFI receive ₦10,000 off the standard ₦50,000 fee and retain Early Bird rates (₦40,000 / $80) even after April 23, 2027.

Can international or remote scholars present online?+

Yes. Full hybrid participation lets online delegates present live via Zoom, receive digital certificates, and qualify for free IJFI publication.
